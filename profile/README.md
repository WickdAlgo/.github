# WickdAlgo

Market structure, made visible.

WickdAlgo is a trading-research project for exploring market structure and turning
ideas into testable strategies. We are building tools for repeatable market
analysis, chart-based inspection and strategy research.

## The project family

The web experience brings research and visualization together. The public
[Wickd CLI](https://github.com/WickdAlgo/wickd-cli) provides a terminal client for
supported platform services. Both are part of the same WickdAlgo product family.

The project is under active development. Research features and planned automation
should not be interpreted as a claim of production-ready live trading or proven
investment performance.

Follow development through the [WickdAlgo organization](https://github.com/WickdAlgo).
Brand assets for this profile are maintained in this repository.

## Disclaimer

WickdAlgo is software for research and market analysis, not financial advice,
a signal service or a promise of returns. Trading involves risk. Users remain
responsible for their strategies, configuration and trading decisions.
